Pizza House Simulator Economy & Stock Market Guide

Beyond pizza, the game hides a living economy. Master it and your restaurant funds itself.

Loans & Banking

You can take loans from the bank to fund expansion. Watch interest and keep a repayment buffer — over-leveraging during a disaster month is a classic trap.

Stock Market Investing

The game includes a stock market. Idle cash can be invested, but prices move with in-game events, so treat it as a side hustle, not your main income early on.

Read the News, Predict Events

Listen to radio broadcasts and read local newspapers to predict future events before the market reacts. A tomato farmer strike today can become tomorrow's buying opportunity.

Ingredient Price Arbitrage

Ingredient prices fluctuate. Buy staples when cheap, and adjust your menu pricing when costs spike. Smart operators turn supply swings into profit.
