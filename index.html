What is Pizza House Simulator?

Pizza House Simulator is a cozy-yet-chaotic pizzeria management sim set on the sunny streets of the Amalfi Coast. You prepare authentic pizzas, manage your kitchen, customize your restaurant, survive rush hours, and turn a struggling family business into a thriving Italian restaurant empire.

From peaceful seaside mornings to absolute dinner-time chaos, every shift tells a different story. The game blends hands-on cooking, automation, a living economy, and optional co-op for up to 6 players.
