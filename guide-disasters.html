Pizza House Simulator Disaster Survival Guide

The game throws random events at your business. Here is what can go wrong and how to survive it.

Types of Disasters

The store description confirms events such as fires, thieves, mafia incidents and unexpected disasters, plus ordinary equipment failure. Each can wreck a shift if ignored.

Fire

Fires often trace back to overloaded or ignored equipment. Keep stations repaired and don't push staff past safe limits.

Thieves & Mafia Incidents

These hit your cash and inventory. A cash buffer and good security habits reduce the damage; some management styles attract more trouble than others.

Equipment Failure

Broken stations slow service and anger customers. Preventive maintenance during quiet hours is cheaper than emergency repairs at peak.

Prevention Mindset

  • Repair proactively, not reactively.
  • Keep a cash buffer for shocks.
  • Match your 'management style' to your risk tolerance.
  • Watch the news — some events are telegraphed.
