Pizza House Simulator Co-op Mode

The kitchen is more fun with friends. Here is how multiplayer, competition and streaming work.

Play With Friends (Up to 6)

Pizza House Simulator supports online co-op for up to 6 players. One player can host while others join; tasks split naturally — someone on dough, someone on the oven, someone on customer service.

Online Co-op & Leaderboards

Compete against other restaurants and climb the leaderboards. Coordinating roles with friends is the quickest path to a top spot, since a 6-player kitchen can out-throughput a solo one dramatically.

Twitch Integration

With Twitch integration you can invite viewers directly into your restaurant and interact with them in real time — great for chaotic live gameplay moments and community builds.

Co-op Tips

  • Assign stations by strength: calm players on the oven, fast typers on orders.
  • Use voice chat — timing matters during rush.
  • Don't all crowd one station; spread out.
