Pizza House Simulator Beginners Guide

Just bought the game? Here is what to focus on in your first hours on the Amalfi Coast so you don't drown in dinner-time chaos.

Your First Shift

Start by learning the core loop: take the order → prep ingredients → stretch dough → bake → serve. Early shifts are calm on purpose — use the quiet morning to learn station layouts before customers pile in.

Master the Kitchen Stations

Each station (dough, toppings, oven, pass) can break down. Keep an eye on the repair prompt and fix stations before the dinner rush. A broken oven during peak hours is the fastest way to lose customers.

Handle the Rush Hour

Dinner time is where the game gets chaotic. Prioritize the longest-waiting customers, batch similar orders, and don't panic-upgrade mid-rush — save big changes for the overnight lull.

Early-Game Tips

  • Don't spend all your money on decorations — upgrade production first.
  • Listen to the radio/newspaper; they hint at upcoming events.
  • Keep one station manual until you can afford reliable automation.
  • Save a cash buffer for disasters and loan interest.
